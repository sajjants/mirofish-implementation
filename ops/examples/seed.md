VERIFIED, ESTIMATE, BELIEF or UNKNOWN; untagged lines are context.

## Background

- VERIFIED: On 3 March 2026 the Riverton City Council published Proposal 14, which would close the Harbor Street bus depot and move its 62 buses to the Northgate depot.
- VERIFIED: Mayor Elena Duarte supports Proposal 14 and says it saves the city money.
- ESTIMATE: The city's finance office estimates savings of 4.2 million dollars over five years.
- VERIFIED: Councillor Tomas Reyes represents the Harbor Street ward and has asked for a public hearing.
- BELIEF: Many residents believe the closure will lengthen bus journeys for night-shift workers.
- VERIFIED: The Riverton Transit Workers Union represents 410 drivers and mechanics at the two depots.
- VERIFIED: The Harbor Street Merchants Association says the depot brings customers to local shops.
- VERIFIED: The Greenway Coalition, an environmental group, wants the depot site turned into a park.
- UNKNOWN: Whether the union will call a strike if Proposal 14 passes.
- UNKNOWN: How Councillor Reyes will vote on Proposal 14.
- VERIFIED: The council vote is scheduled for 21 April 2026. The outcome is not known.

## Local press

- VERIFIED: The Riverton Daily newspaper reported that a petition against the closure has 1,800 signatures.
